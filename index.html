Intro / Elevator Pitch

3rd Year · B.Sc. (Hons) Blockchain Technology, SPPU · 8.5 CGPA · Pune (Remote-friendly) · Open to Internships in Blockchain, MERN & Web Development

I am a passionate software enthusiast deeply fascinated by the architecture of decentralized networks and immutable logic.

My journey into technology is driven by the desire to build trustless applications that eliminate middlemen and empower users globally. What draws me specifically to blockchain is the idea that trust can be engineered into a system itself, rather than placed in institutions — that's what pulled me from general web development into Web3. Whether it's writing secure Solidity smart contracts, optimizing gas consumption, or connecting backend ledgers to sleek frontend interfaces, I enjoy the entire lifecycle of Web3 development.

This philosophy shaped Horizon, a cross-border remittance dApp I built end-to-end on the Stellar Testnet to make international money transfers faster and cheaper for everyday users. I further put these skills to the test in Stellar's Journey to Mastery program (Feb–Apr 2026), where I earned 65+ USDC by building and demonstrating real blockchain solutions in a live, competitive environment.
